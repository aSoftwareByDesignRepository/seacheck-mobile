--- Description (max 4000) ---
SeaCheck is a personal coastal navigation companion for small-boat sailing — a deck-side aid to paper charts, not a replacement for official nautical charts.

Who it is for
• Coastal sailors who want GPS on a chart, passage sketches, and anchor alarms without a cloud account
• Skippers who download OpenSeaMap regions on Wi‑Fi for offline use underway

What you need
• An Android or iPhone device with GPS
• Optional: Wi‑Fi to download offline chart packs
• No sign-up, no Nextcloud server, no license key

What the app does
• GPS on chart — COG, SOG, bearing
• Passage planning, waypoints, GPX export
• Offline region packs (OpenSeaMap base + seamarks)
• Optional online depth overlay (GEBCO WMS — online only)
• Anchor, arrival, and off-course alarms
• Track recording
• English and German UI

Important — not for primary navigation
Aid to navigation only. Maps use OpenSeaMap / OpenStreetMap volunteer data and may be outdated. Always carry official, corrected nautical charts.

Privacy
• No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
• Passages, tracks, and GPS history stay on your device
• Online map tile and optional Overpass lookups may reveal map areas you view (see privacy policy)
